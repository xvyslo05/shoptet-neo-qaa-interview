1. Ukazat fci parseDate v backend/src/engine.ts
Dotaz: 
- co je vstup, co muze byt vystup
- kdy fce vraci null: priklad
- kdy vraci match (den,mnesic): priklad

Czech forms such as 7.3., 7.3, 07.03., 7.3.2024, and 7. 3. 2024 match, and so does ISO 2024-03-07. Anything else, including not-a-date and -1.5., is null because neither pattern matches.
A day or month that cannot exist, such as 32.1. or 31.4., is also null. February is allowed up to the 29th at this step.
The year is checked only for 29 February. 29.2. has no year, so it is accepted. 29.2.2024 and 29.2.2000 are accepted. 29.2.2023 and 29.2.1900 are null.
